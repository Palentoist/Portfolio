About

I'm a frontend engineer who likes the space where design meets code. I care about accessible, well-structured interfaces and the small details that make a product feel finished: focus states, loading states, the way a layout behaves at 320 pixels wide.

Most recently I've worked on a component library and the product surfaces built from it, pairing closely with designers and backend engineers. Before that I shipped marketing sites, dashboards and mobile web apps for startups and agencies. I'm happiest when a team can move quickly because the foundations are solid.

Away from the keyboard I read, take long walks and slowly improve my chess. Right now I'm exploring design tokens and how they travel from Figma to production.
